About the PCC Library

Pentecostal Catholic Communion

The Pentecostal Catholic Communion (PCC) is a sacramental Christian communion rooted in the historic faith of the Church, nourished by the catholic tradition, and renewed in the living presence of the Holy Spirit.

Purpose of This Library

This library serves as the authoritative digital repository of the PCC’s liturgical, doctrinal, and constitutional texts. Nothing contained herein is secret or reserved.

Core Texts

  • Missal — The Order of Mass and Eucharistic rites
  • Breviary — Daily prayer of the Church
  • Rituale — Sacramental and pastoral rites
  • Catechism — Doctrinal teaching of the PCC
  • Constitution — Governance and ecclesial identity
