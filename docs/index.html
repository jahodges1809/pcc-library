# Pentecostal Catholic Communion **Library of the Pentecostal Catholic Communion** Welcome to the public liturgical and doctrinal library of the PCC. Here are preserved the texts that govern our worship, prayer, and life: - The Holy Eucharist (Missal) - The Daily Office (Breviary) - The Sacraments and Rites (Rituale) - The Catechism of the Great Hope - The Apostolic Constitution These texts are offered freely, in reverence, and in service to the Church universal.
