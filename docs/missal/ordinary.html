Ordinary of the Mass

SECTION I

PRAYERS AT THE FOOT OF THE HIGH ALTAR

(Before the Public Beginning of Mass)

These prayers serve as the Presider’s personal and ministerial preparation before the public celebration of the Sacred Mysteries. They do not replace the public Penitential Act, but deepen it.

Presider, standing at the foot of the High Altar, facing the altar. General Server, kneeling slightly behind and to the Presider’s right. Other servers remain in the sacristy or assigned places.

OPENING SILENT PREPARATION

The Presider stands recollected, hands joined.

O God, who dwell in unapproachable light, cleanse my heart and mind, that I may approach your holy mysteries with reverence, humility, and love, and serve you faithfully for the good of your people.

PSALM 43 (42) — Judica me, Deus

(Normative on Sundays and Solemnities)

Judge me, O God, and discern my cause from the nation that is not holy; deliver me from the unjust and deceitful.

For you are the God of my strength; why have you cast me off? Why do I go sorrowful while the enemy oppresses me?

Send forth your light and your truth; they have led me and brought me to your holy hill and to your dwelling place.

And I will go to the altar of God, to God who gives joy to my youth.

I will praise you upon the harp, O God, my God. Why are you sad, O my soul, and why do you trouble me?

Hope in God, for I will yet praise him, the salvation of my countenance and my God.

Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

PCC CONFITEOR

(Preparatory, Ministerial Form)

All kneel.

I confess to the One God, source of all mercy and truth, that I have sinned in thought, word, deed, and in what I have failed to do.

I have trusted myself more than grace, and turned from love when I should have remained.

Therefore I ask the faithful, and the communion of saints, to pray for me to the Lord our God.

May the Almighty and Merciful God grant you healing, forgiveness, and renewal of heart.

Amen.

PRAYER FOR WORTHY SERVICE

The Presider bows deeply.

Take from me, O Lord, my iniquities, that I may enter your holy sanctuary with a pure mind and serve you worthily.

ASCENT AND VENERATION OF THE HIGH ALTAR

The Presider ascends the steps of the High Altar. He kisses the altar reverently.

If incense is used at the High Altar: The General Server presents the incense boat. The Presider imposes incense silently. The altar is incensed with measured solemnity. The Presider pauses briefly in silent prayer.

TRANSITION TO THE PUBLIC MASS

The Presider descends from the High Altar. Servers move to form the Entrance Procession. The Mass Proper will now begin at the Main Altar with the Entrance Rite.

Section II — Entrance Procession & Beginning of Mass

The Presider remains standing at the chair. The people stand.

Penitential Act

The Presider invites the people to acknowledge their sins, trusting not in fear, but in the healing mercy of God.

Beloved of God, let us call to mind our need for healing, and prepare ourselves to celebrate the sacred mysteries.

PCC Confiteor (Public Form)

All bow slightly.

I confess to the One God, source of mercy and truth, that I have sinned in thought, word, and deed, and in what I have failed to do.

I have trusted myself more than grace, and turned from love when I should have remained.

Therefore I ask the communion of saints, and you, People of God, to pray for me to the Lord our God.

Presider: May the Almighty and Merciful God grant us healing, forgiveness of sins, and renewal of life through Jesus Christ.

All: Amen.

Kyrie (PCC Form)

The Kyrie follows the general absolution and is ordinarily sung or said.

Lord Jesus, you were sent to heal the contrite of heart:

All: Lord, have mercy.

Lord Jesus, you came to call sinners into the covenant of life:

All: Christ, have mercy.

Lord Jesus, you intercede for us at the right hand of the Father:

All: Lord, have mercy.

Gloria

On Sundays outside Advent and Lent, and on solemnities, the Gloria is sung or said.

Gloria in excelsis Deo,

et in terra pax hominibus bonae voluntatis.

Laudamus te, benedicimus te, adoramus te, glorificamus te,

gratias agimus tibi propter magnam gloriam tuam,

Domine Deus, Rex caelestis, Deus Pater omnipotens.

Domine Fili unigenite, Iesu Christe,

Domine Deus, Agnus Dei, Filius Patris,

qui tollis peccata mundi, miserere nobis;

qui tollis peccata mundi, suscipe deprecationem nostram;

qui sedes ad dexteram Patris, miserere nobis.

Quoniam tu solus Sanctus, tu solus Dominus,

tu solus Altissimus, Iesu Christe,

cum Sancto Spiritu in gloria Dei Patris.

Amen.

PCC English Translation

Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to people of goodwill.

We praise you, we bless you, we adore you, we glorify you, we give you thanks for your great glory, Lord God, heavenly King, O God, almighty Father.

Lord Jesus Christ, Only Begotten Son, Lord God, Lamb of God, Son of the Father, you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us; you take away the sins of the world, receive our prayer; you are seated at the right hand of the Father, have mercy on us.

For you alone are the Holy One, you alone are the Lord, you alone are the Most High, Jesus Christ, with the Holy Spirit, in the glory of God the Father.

Amen.

The Collect

The Presider extends hands. The people remain standing.

Let us pray.

O God, who call your people into covenant not by fear but by love, gather us into the unity of your Spirit, that healed and made whole, we may offer you a living sacrifice of praise. Through Jesus Christ our Lord, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, one God, for ever and ever.

All: Amen.

Section III — Liturgy of the Word

First Reading

The Reader, a member of the congregation, rises from their place. The Reader approaches the sanctuary, bows deeply to the altar, and proceeds to the ambo.

The reading is proclaimed from the Douay–Rheims Bible (public domain), according to the Proper of the Day.

Reader: The word of the Lord.

All: Thanks be to God.

Responsorial Psalm

The Psalm is sung or recited.

Second Reading

The Reader again approaches the ambo, bows to the altar, and proclaims the Second Reading from the Douay–Rheims Bible, according to the Proper of the Day.

Reader: The word of the Lord.

All: Thanks be to God.

Gospel Acclamation

All stand.

Alleluia, alleluia.

(Proper verse may be sung.)

Alleluia.

Gospel Procession

The Thurifer approaches the Presider. The General Server presents the incense boat. The Presider imposes incense silently.

The Gospel Book is carried in procession into the nave, accompanied by the Thurifer and Torchbearers.

Proclamation of the Holy Gospel

Presider: The Lord be with you.

All: And with your spirit.

Presider: A reading from the Holy Gospel according to N.

All: Glory to you, O Lord.

All trace the triple sign of the cross on forehead, lips, and heart.

The Gospel is proclaimed from the Douay–Rheims Bible, according to the Proper of the Day.

Presider: The Gospel of the Lord.

All: Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.

The Presider kisses the Gospel Book.

Homily

The people sit. The Presider gives the homily.

Profession of Faith

All stand.

All: We believe in one God, the Father almighty, maker of heaven and earth, of all things visible and invisible.

All: We believe in one Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten Son of God…

(Continue the Nicene–Constantinopolitan Creed as approved in the PCC Missal; without the filioque.)

Universal Prayer (Prayer of the Faithful)

The people kneel where able; sitting is permitted.

Presider: Trusting in the mercy of the One God, let us bring our prayers before him.

Intercessions are proclaimed according to the Proper or PCC form. After each petition:

All: Lord, hear our prayer.

The Presider concludes:

Presider: Merciful Father, accept these prayers for the sake of your Son, our Savior Jesus Christ.

All: Amen.

Section IV — Peace & Preparation of the Gifts

Sign of Peace (Before the Gifts)

All stand.

Beloved of God, our Lord Jesus Christ said to his Apostles:

“If you are offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother or sister has something against you, leave your gift there before the altar, go first and be reconciled, and then come and offer your gift.”

Lord Jesus Christ, you said to your Apostles, “My peace I leave you, my peace I give you.” Look not upon our sins, but upon the faith of your Church, and graciously grant her peace and unity in accordance with your will, who live and reign for ever and ever.

All: Amen.

The peace of the Lord be always with you.

All: And with your spirit.

Let us offer one another some sign of Christ’s peace.

The Presider may exchange a sign of peace with the servers. The faithful exchange peace in a restrained and reverent manner, without leaving their places unnecessarily.

Preparation of the Gifts

After the Sign of Peace:

The Crucifer moves to the nave to receive monetary offerings. Torchbearers assist in receiving the bread and wine from the people. The General Server remains near the Presider.

The altar is prepared. The gifts are brought forward.

Presider: Blessed are you, Lord God of all creation…

All: Blessed be God forever.

The Presider washes hands (Lavabo).

Presider: Pray, beloved of God, that this sacrifice, which we offer together, may be acceptable to God, the Almighty Father.

All: May the Lord accept the sacrifice at your hands for the praise and glory of his name, for our good and the good of all his holy Church.

Prayer over the Offerings (Proper or Ordinary) is prayed.

All: Amen.

Section V — Eucharistic Prayer

The Presider turns ad orientem at the altar.

Preface Dialogue

Presider: The Lord be with you.

All: And with your spirit.

Presider: Lift up your hearts.

All: We lift them up to the Lord.

Presider: Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.

All: It is right and just.

The Presider prays the normative Preface of Eucharistic Prayer IV.

Sanctus

A deep bow is made until the first Hosanna.

All: Sanctus, Sanctus, Sanctus Dominus Deus Sabaoth…

All kneel.

Eucharistic Prayer IV (Ordinary)

The Presider prays Eucharistic Prayer IV in full, as promulgated in the PCC Missal.

Bells: one long ring at the epiclesis. Three long rings at each elevation. One long ring at the doxology.

At each elevation: the Presider genuflects before the elevation and genuflects again after the elevation.

Doxology

All: Amen.

Section VI — Communion Rite

All remain kneeling where able.

The Lord’s Prayer

Presider: At the Savior’s command and formed by divine teaching, we dare to say:

All: Our Father, who art in heaven…

Fraction & Agnus Dei

The Host is broken reverently.

All: Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world…

Invitation to Communion

Presider: Behold the Lamb of God…

All: Lord, I am not worthy…

Communion of the Faithful

The faithful approach the altar rail and kneel to receive the Body and Blood of Christ.

Presider: The Body of Christ.

Communicant: Amen.

Presider: The Blood of Christ.

Communicant: Amen.

Silence after Communion

The faithful may sit or kneel in silent prayer. It is appropriate to resume sitting when the altar has been cleared.

Prayer after Communion

Presider: Let us pray.

Prayer after Communion (Proper or Ordinary) is prayed.

All: Amen.

Section VII — Concluding Rites

Solemn Blessing (Ordinary)

The people kneel or bow their heads.

Presider: Bow your heads and pray for God’s blessing.

Presider: May the One God, who brought light out of darkness and life out of death, bless you and keep you.

All: Amen.

Presider: May the Lord make his face shine upon you, heal what is wounded, and strengthen what is weary.

All: Amen.

Presider: May the Lord turn his countenance toward you, grant you peace in your hearts, justice in your communities, and hope for all creation.

All: Amen.

Presider: And may the blessing of Almighty God, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, descend upon you and remain with you always.

All: Amen.

Dismissal

Presider: Go forth in peace, filled with the Spirit, to love and serve the Lord.

All: Thanks be to God.
